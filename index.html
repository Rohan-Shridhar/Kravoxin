🚀 Explore Your Clipboard

Kravoxin

A lightweight Chrome extension that captures and organizes your clipboard history. Never lose a copied snippet again.

Get Started View on GitHub

Features

📋

Auto Capture

Every text you copy on a webpage is automatically saved to your history with zero effort required.

💾

Local Storage

All your clipboard history is stored securely on your device in chrome.storage.local. No cloud sync, complete privacy.

⚡

Lightning Fast

Minimal footprint. Instant access to your clipboard history through the clean, intuitive popup interface.

❤️

Favorite Items

Mark important snippets as favorites to keep them safe from bulk deletions.

🗑️

Easy Deletion

Remove unwanted items instantly or clear all non-favorite entries with one click.

🎨

Modern Design

Beautiful dark theme with glassmorphism effects. A pleasure to look at and use.

Installation

1

Download

Clone or download the Kravoxin repository from GitHub.

2

Open Extensions

Navigate to chrome://extensions/ in your Chrome browser.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Toggle the Developer mode switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

4

Load Unpacked

Click "Load unpacked" and select the Kravoxin folder from your computer.

5

Start Using

Copy text anywhere on the web. Click the Kravoxin icon to view your clipboard history!
