    01 THE IDEA

    A LITTLE LESS RETRACING

    The clipboard is a workbench, not a revolving door.

    You copy a command, switch tabs, and keep moving. Later, Kravoxin helps you find it again without asking you to remember where you saw it—or sending it somewhere else.

    Copied text is collected from web pages and kept in your browser’s local storage. Search your history, pin the items you rely on, and copy them again when the moment comes.

    Made for the ordinary, easily lost fragments of a working day.

    Nothing to sync.
    Nothing to send.

    03 GOOD TO KNOW

    A few practical details.

    Which browsers can run Kravoxin?

    Kravoxin is built for modern Chromium-based browsers that support Manifest V3, including Chrome, Brave, and Microsoft Edge.

    Where is my clipboard history stored?

    In your browser’s local extension storage. The landing-page preview is separate and does not persist any sample data.

    Can I inspect or contribute to the project?

    Yes. The source and contribution information are available in the public GitHub repository.
